Early Access

What to expect

  • FlowScript is usable today, but it's still being built. Expect rough edges, missing polish and the occasional bug.
  • Features can change between builds as they get refined, so keep backups of files you care about.
  • New builds land often. FlowScript checks for updates and tells you when one is out.
  • Early Access builds are free to download and use.

Help shape it

  • Found a bug or something that feels wrong? Tell us. Early Access feedback decides what gets fixed first.
  • Ideas for nodes, file types or workflows are welcome too.
  • New to FlowScript? The tutorial opens on first launch, QuickStart is in the Help menu, and the docs cover the rest.
  • Paid licenses arrive with version 1.0. Until then, Early Access is how FlowScript gets tested in the real world.
